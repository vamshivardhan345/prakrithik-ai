INVESTIGATION OF ROOT CAUSES:
1. High Visual and Morphological Similarity:
   - Diseases like Tomato Early Blight and Tomato Target Spot produce very similar brown necrotic spots with concentric halos on foliage.
   - Corn Cercospora Leaf Spot and Corn Northern Leaf Blight both produce elongated grey-to-brown lesions on maize foliage that overlap in early stages.
2. Class Imbalance:
   - Some classes (e.g. Potato healthy, Tomato mosaic virus) have only 150-370 samples, compared to over 5,000 for Orange Huanglongbing or Soybean healthy.
   - Despite stratified splitting, smaller minority classes have fewer gradient signals during training.
3. Pre-harvest and Background Variance:
   - Subtle color variations due to illumination or camera sensor differences in laboratory conditions occasionally affect fine-grained symptom differentiation.
