Methodology & Ethics

About the Prakritik Project

Bridging cutting-edge edge AI with sustainable, chemical-free organic agriculture.

MobileNetV2 Architecture

We employ a deep inverted residual MobileNetV2 network pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on the 54,305-image PlantVillage dataset. The model balances lightweight CPU inference efficiency with high multi-class diagnostic precision across 38 distinct crop conditions.

Why Pure Organic Farming?

Excessive chemical pesticides degrade the soil microbiome, contaminate groundwater tables, and trigger pesticide resistance. Our platform exclusively serves verified bio-control agents (e.g. Trichoderma, Bacillus, Bt) and cultural sanitation practices.

Extension & Label Compliance

Every recommendation is checked against agricultural guidelines (ICAR / State Agricultural Universities). Generic unverified chemical doses are never invented; farmers are always guided to check certified commercial product labels.
